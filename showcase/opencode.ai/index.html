## FAQ
−Is OpenCode really free?
Yes — the client is open source under MIT. Bring your own model or subscribe to OpenCode Zen.
+Which providers are supported?
All major providers, plus any local model via Ollama.
+Does it work with my editor?
TUI everywhere, plus VS Code and JetBrains extensions, and a headless SDK for automation.
