[?] FAQ

+Is OpenCode really free?

Yes. The agent is open source under MIT. Zen and Enterprise are paid services for hosted models and support.

+Which models can I bring?

Any. OpenCode ships with provider presets for Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok, Qwen, and any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.

−Does my code leave my machine?

Only the context you send to your chosen model provider. Local-only routing keeps every byte on-device.
